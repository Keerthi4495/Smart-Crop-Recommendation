What to do while identification is unavailable

  1. Take one close, clear photo of the affected area and another showing the whole plant.
  2. Note the crop, when symptoms started, how they are spreading, and recent weather, watering, and fertilizer use.
  3. Show the photos and notes to your local agriculture officer, extension service, or qualified crop expert for identification and crop-specific management advice.
  4. Do not apply a pesticide or fungicide based only on this photo. For any registered treatment, follow its label and local expert advice.

Disease identification is a guidance tool. Confirm serious or uncertain cases with a qualified agricultural expert before applying chemical treatments.
